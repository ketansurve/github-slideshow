Frequently Asked Questions

How do I view or download a Commission report?

Public reports are listed on the Reports page and in the Document Archive; click "View" against any document marked Public. Restricted and Confidential documents require sign-in and Commission approval — see the Document Archive page.

How do I get access to the Document Archive?

Government users sign in through Jan Parichay / Bihar SSO. Advocates and researchers register and wait for approval by the Commission before their first sign-in. This demo site simulates the sign-in flow on the Login page.

Can I switch the site to Hindi?

Yes — use the English / हिन्दी toggle in the utility bar at the top of every page. Your choice is remembered for later visits.

How do I report a problem with the website?

Use the Feedback page, or write to lawcommission-bih@nic.in.

Screen Reader Access

This website follows WCAG 2.1 Level AA practices: a "Skip to Main Content" link at the top of every page, semantic headings and landmarks, labelled form fields, and visible keyboard focus outlines. It has been checked with NVDA and VoiceOver in the course of building this demo.

Free screen reader software: NVDA (Windows) and Narrator (Windows) are built in or free to download; VoiceOver is built into macOS and iOS; TalkBack is built into Android.
