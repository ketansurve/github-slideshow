User Login

Authorised access only

  • Government users may sign in through Jan Parichay / Bihar SSO.
  • Advocates and researchers must register and be approved by the Commission before first sign-in.
  • A one-time password is sent to the registered mobile number at every sign-in.
  • Accounts lock after five failed attempts.

Unauthorised access to this system is an offence under the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Front-end demo: enter any User ID and Password, solve the captcha, request the OTP (shown on screen) and sign in — no real account is required.
